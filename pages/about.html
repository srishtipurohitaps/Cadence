ABOUT

Keep the rhythm.

Keyboard shortcuts available below. Mouse and pointer usage disabled.

Cadence is a little record of everything you finish.

Books, Movies, Shows, Games, Albums. Whatever you spend your time with, you can log it, rate it, and look back on it later.

THE IDEA

More than a list.

Most media trackers are built around collecting things you want to consume.

Cadence is about the other side of that. The things you've actually finished.

Every entry becomes a part of a bigger picture, which is, what you've been into, how consistently you've been finishing things, and how your rhythm changes over time.
